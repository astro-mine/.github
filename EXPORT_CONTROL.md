# Export Control & Dual-Use Notice

Astro-Mine is space and robotics technology, and the project takes export control and
dual-use seriously **from day one**. Open does not mean naive.

## Posture

- **The open core is science, simulation, and coordination.** Astro-Mine open-sources the
  shared simulation, benchmark, and orchestration commons — physical and learned models,
  research environments, and coordination algorithms.
- **Operational targeting is out of scope.** Anything resembling a certification-grade
  flight-code generator or turnkey operational targeting for real flight hardware is
  **intentionally excluded** from the open core. Capabilities that are genuinely sensitive
  are partitioned rather than published.
- **Interop, not weaponization.** Bridges to ROS 2, cFS, F´, SPICE, OpenMCT, and STK/GMAT
  exist to lower adoption cost for research and education, not to deliver operational
  capability against real assets.

## Your responsibility as a contributor or user

- **Comply with applicable law.** Depending on your jurisdiction, regulations such as the
  U.S. **ITAR** (22 CFR 120–130) and **EAR** (15 CFR 730–774), or equivalent national
  controls, may apply to space and dual-use technology. You are responsible for your own
  compliance when using, contributing to, or redistributing this software.
- **Do not contribute controlled material.** Do not submit code, data, or documentation
  that is subject to export controls or that you are not authorized to release publicly
  (e.g., controlled technical data, ITAR-controlled designs, proprietary mission data).
- **Flag concerns early.** If a proposed contribution might cross into controlled or
  sensitive operational capability, raise it in an issue **before** writing code.

## Disclaimer

This notice is informational and is **not legal advice**. It does not constitute a
determination of the export-control classification of any part of this project. If you are
unsure whether your use or contribution is subject to export controls, consult qualified
counsel.
